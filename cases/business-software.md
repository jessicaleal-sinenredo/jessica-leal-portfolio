# Business Software for Hospitality & Administration

**Role:** Business process analysis, product design, software development and engineering collaboration.

My operational background in hospitality informs the software I build. I have contributed to applications for hotel workflows and administrative processes, including **Arribo** (hotel property-management software) and **Listo** (administrative and electronic invoicing workflows).

## What this work demonstrates

- Translating real business rules into workflows, screens, and data structures.
- Building with React/TypeScript and backend APIs and data services.
- Working with structured records, business operations and integrations.
- Prioritizing correctness, security reviews and iterative testing over feature claims.

## Boundaries

These are ongoing software projects. This portfolio does **not** assert that every feature has been deployed, independently security-certified, or adopted by third-party customers. Production code and customer information remain private.
