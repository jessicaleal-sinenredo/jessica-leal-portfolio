# Appointment Scheduling & Reminders — Service Business

**Role:** Automation and integration development within a collaborative business solution.

**Business need:** A service provider needed appointment information to stay consistent between their calendar and customer communications, with timely reminders that did not require a staff member to contact each customer manually.

**Solution areas:**

- Integration with Google Calendar for scheduling workflows.
- Automated reminders tied to appointment records.
- State reconciliation and rules to avoid duplicate reminders.
- Operational controls for exceptions and human attention.

**Technologies:** APIs, webhooks, scheduling logic, Google Calendar and business messaging integrations.

**Outcome:** Scheduling and reminders were implemented for an operational pilot. No quantified time savings or conversion improvements are stated without measurement.

**Confidentiality:** Client identity and production records are omitted. This case describes an integration pattern, not a public code release.
